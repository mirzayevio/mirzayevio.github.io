## Profiling Python: from cProfile to sampling

**Swiss Python Summit 2026** · Conference talk

What does a profiler actually measure? A closer look at tracing, sampling, and why the difference matters.

Tracing records calls and returns. Sampling takes snapshots of the stack. They answer different questions, and the overhead of tracing can change which part of a program appears slowest.

The talk moves from `cProfile` to sampling, exploring call counts, runtime shares, and flame graphs through a small Python workload.
